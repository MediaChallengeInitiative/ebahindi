## 5. Templated filler (the specific problems to fix)

1. **Meta description is literally `"Generated by create next app"`** — `components/commonHead/index.js:19`.
   This is what Google and every WhatsApp/LinkedIn link preview currently shows.
2. **Photoshop, Illustrator and "diamond" icons float around the portrait**
   (`hero.js` → `images/icon/photoshop.svg`, `illustrator.svg`, `diamond.svg`).
   He is a software engineer. These are design-agency template assets and are simply wrong.
3. **Skill percentage bars** — "PHP & Laravel 98%", "React JS & Next JS 88%", etc.
   The numbers are also internally inconsistent: `data-count="92"` renders as `100%`,
   `data-count="72"` renders as `95%`, `data-count="43"` renders as `100%`. Meaningless.
4. **"100% Clients Satisfections"** counter — cliché *and* misspelled.
5. **Auto-playing project carousel** hides most of the work and fights mobile scroll.
6. **"03 Years of Experience"** — stale; the brief states 5+ years, 30+ projects.
7. **Wrong contact email** — site shows `ebahindi@gmail.com`; correct is `bahindiemmanuel20@gmail.com`.
8. **CTA is "Hire Me"** — the freelancer-for-hire framing the redesign exists to replace.
9. **Bootstrap loaded twice** — once via npm in `_app.js`, again from a jsDelivr CDN `<link>`
   in `commonHead`. Two full copies of Bootstrap CSS on every page load.
10. **Three icon fonts** — Font Awesome, Themify, and Flaticon, all loaded in full for a
    handful of glyphs.

## 6. The contact form is decorative

`components/ContactFrom/ContactForm.js` — `submitHandler` calls `e.preventDefault()`,
runs validation, then **clears the fields and returns**. There is no `fetch`, no API route,
no mail service. Every enquiry submitted through this site has been silently discarded.
The subject dropdown offers "Web Design / Web Development / **Marketing**" — services he
does not offer.

## 9. Accessibility & SEO

- **Every `<img>` has `alt=""`** — portrait, logos, project thumbnails, all of them.
- No `<main>`, no `<section>` labelling, no skip link. Heading order jumps `h2` → `h5`.
- No `lang` attribute set beyond the Next.js default.
- No Open Graph or Twitter card tags → link previews on WhatsApp and LinkedIn, the primary
  channels for this audience, render bare.
- Favicon is the default Next.js `favicon.ico`.
- Clickable `<h2>` wrapping a `<Link>` in the carousel; `alt="_blank"` on a `Link`
  (`ProjectSection.js`) — an invalid prop that does nothing.

## 11. Summary — what carries over

**Keep:** Next.js, Vercel deployment, the portrait, the MCI/Baylor logos, the real
project URLs, the phone number, the scroll-aware nav idea.

**Replace:** every section's markup and copy, the whole SCSS layer, the routing of the
contact path, all metadata.

**Delete:** 9 unused packages, `react-router-dom`, MUI, slick, Bootstrap, the validator,
5 unused components, 2 fixture files, the `/api/hello` scaffold, ~28 MB of template stock
imagery, 58 font files, and the three icon fonts.
